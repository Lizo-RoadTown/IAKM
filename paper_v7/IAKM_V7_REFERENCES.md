**Recency, addressed 2026-09-09.** Before this pass, nothing in the list was newer than 2023, which invites the reviewer question of whether the gap is still open. Entries [14] through [17] were added to Section 2 for that reason. The turnover literature was left at Galan 2023 [5], where a 2023 source is still current for the field; the recency pressure is on the AI side, where the state of the art moves fastest and a reviewer will ask whether LLM repository mining has already closed the gap. Two were corrected on the way in: [17] had an incomplete title and [19] had the wrong title entirely, both on DOIs that resolve, which is the failure mode a spot check does not catch. [16] is an unrefereed preprint and is the only one in the list; it is cited alongside the peer-reviewed [17] and carries no claim on its own. Both AI entries are fragile by nature: [16] has no venue and [17] is online-first with no volume, issue, or pages yet. Neither should ever carry a load-bearing claim, and in Section 2 neither does.

Three entries carry bibliographic verification without a content read: Argote, Lave and Wenger, and Hjørland and Albrechtsen. None carries a figure or a specific finding in V7, so each supports only a general characterization of its own field. That is the weakest acceptable standing for a citation, and it is worth closing before submission if the sources are obtainable.

**Note on [9].** Hjørland 2002 carried a second, stronger use in an earlier version, asserting that the paper contains procedures for declaring and bounding a domain. That sentence was reworded to say IAKM adopts none of the eleven approaches as a fixed procedure, which is what the verified record supports. ## The Simon, Pimmler, Browning attribution, now split in the text

Section 2 previously carried all three behind one sentence. It now runs one claim per citation:

| Sentence | Source, and why it carries that part |
|---|---|
| Complex systems decompose into subsystems whose internal interactions are stronger than the interactions between them | Simon 1962, near-decomposability. Confirmed in the text: interactions at different levels "may be, and often will be, of different orders of magnitude" |
| Interfaces between components carry types and differential magnitude, so a pair is described by a vector rather than by a single value | Pimmler and Eppinger 1994. Four interaction types, spatial, energy, information, material, and the phrase "vector of four scores" is verbatim in the source. Magnitude is a five-point signed scale from required at plus two to detrimental at minus two |
| The design structure matrix tradition has extended that representation across several lineages | Browning 2016 |

**One limit to keep.** Pimmler and Eppinger supports type and magnitude and **does not support direction**. Their matrix is undirected. Direction in the DSM tradition comes from the activity-based lineage, where sequence makes feedforward and feedback meaningful, and not from the component-based work this citation points to. V7 does not claim direction, which is correct. Do not let the word back in during a later edit.
