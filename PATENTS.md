# Patent Non-Assertion Pledge

**IAKM — Interface-Anchored Knowledge Mapping**
Elizabeth Osborn, 2026

## The pledge

I will not assert any patent right I hold or may come to hold against any
person or organization for implementing, practicing, teaching, adapting, or
commercializing the IAKM methodology, or any system implementing it, as
described in this repository and in the papers it contains — both the
methodology paper and the implementation design case.

This pledge is irrevocable and extends to all users, for any purpose,
including commercial purposes, with no requirement of permission, notice,
payment, or reciprocity.

## Why this exists

This work is published deliberately and in full, including material that goes
beyond any earlier patent filing. That is intentional. The methodology is
meant to be usable by anyone who finds it useful, and to remain that way.

This document exists so that intent is stated rather than inferred. A silent
decision not to pursue a patent leaves everyone else guessing; a written
pledge is something people can rely on when deciding whether to build on this
work.

## Published as prior art

This work is published as **prior art**, with the intent that the IAKM
methodology remain free for all to use.

Public, dated, archival publication is what prevents *any* party — including
parties unrelated to the author — from later obtaining valid patent claims
over material disclosed here. The archived record and its DOI establish the
date and content of that disclosure.

The purpose is not merely that the author declines to enclose this work. It
is that the work not be enclosable.
